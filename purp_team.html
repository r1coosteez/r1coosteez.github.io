Purple Team Simulation

In this simulation, I demonstrated a basic Purple Team exercise. The Red Team system (10.0.2.5) performs a TCP port scan against a Blue Team system (10.0.2.6). I analyze the network traffic in Wireshark to identify open and closed ports and observed how scanning activity appears in packet captures. This demo shows the attack, detection, and defensive workflow in a controlled lab environment.
